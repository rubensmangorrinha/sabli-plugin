# Review a piece of work with an expert's mind

1. Get the work as text: pasted, or the relevant part of a file they shared. Remove other people's personal data first.
2. Pick the mind that knows this kind of work (named by the person, or via `match_minds` with a one-line description of the work).
3. Ask what they want the review to focus on, if they haven't said (clarity, pricing, onboarding, structure…). Pass it as `rubric`.
4. Call `critique`. Work over about 4,000 characters is read as a document and uses extra credits; say so before sending something long.
5. Show the critique as returned, with its attribution block. Then offer a short checklist of the changes it suggests, in order of impact, so they can act on it.
