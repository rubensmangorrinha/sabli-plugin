# Get a second opinion

1. Pick two minds with `match_minds`, or the two the person names. Tell them this asks two questions (two answers' worth of credits) and wait for a yes.
2. Send the same question and context to each with `consult`.
3. Show both answers, each with its own attribution block, one after the other.
4. Then add a short comparison in your own words, clearly marked as yours: where they agree, where they differ, and what each difference depends on. Don't pick a winner for them.
